Parser: month-first parsing of day-first dates

The shared parser reads dates month first even for tenants whose locale writes the day first. For example, R-41200 (harbour.dental, app) describes 7 April becoming 4 July. Use the tenant directory and channel to place the report, not the browser locale or intake tag. The majority cause belongs to #4471; keep it separate from the form-hint defect below. Engineering should make the shared parsing contract agree with the tenant date format and verify ambiguous day/month inputs through app, email, and the older booking form.

Hint: booking-v3 gives day-first tenants a month-first hint

The booking-v3 date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, while the form interprets input in the tenant’s local date order. This is a separate defect for form reports from day-first tenants using booking-v3. R-41230 (copper.studio, form) and R-41251 (pale.harbour, form) belong here even though intake tagged them parser. Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour use this form in the export. Correct the hint and its input contract; a shared-parser fix alone does not resolve this mismatch.

Evidence limits

The computed footnote identifies reports describing a one-day move. The desk’s provided classifier places them in the parser group by tenant and channel. Preserve that count, but investigate their original scheduling/time-zone evidence before claiming a separate time-zone defect or a confirmed mechanism. These are reported symptoms, not reproduced product failures. Do not invent a third cause from wording alone.
